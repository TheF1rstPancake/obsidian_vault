# Voice anchor — what to learn from this sample

This sample is high-signal because it's the **same author writing in a personal/performative register** as the LinkedIn posts. The structural moves are consistent across both contexts — strong evidence for the user's self-description that they write the same way personally and professionally.

## Structural moves that show up here AND in the LinkedIn samples

- **Opening with a callback or generalized observation, then landing it personally.** Vows open with "There was a point in time where you used to look at multi-year engagements and go 'yuck.'" Same shape as the LinkedIn meal-planner opener.
- **Conditional reasoning as the explicit spine.** "In order to continue this successful partnership: I promise to..." The "if X then Y" pattern the user flagged is literally how the list is introduced.
- **Quotable closing inversion.** "I look forward to no longer being a liar when I tell people you are my wife" — the inversion *is* the joke. Same family of move as "AI coding agents handle the syntax. You handle the outcomes."
- **Personality through concrete specifics, not adjectives.** "Donut vodka into Pepsi turns into vanilla Pepsi." "Running shoes if you do not have any intention of running." "And even Target." Never says "I was a goofy kid" — shows it through what 21-year-old Gio was actually doing.
- **Repetition for emphasis in place of adverbs.** "I promise to try really, really, really hard." Pattern is closer to spoken cadence than written intensifiers.

## Moves more visible in personal register

- **Self-deprecation through third-person reference.** "21 year-old Gio had a lot of room for growth." Creates comic distance from the speaker — usable trick when an article needs to be self-critical without becoming earnest.
- **Compressed compound sentences with rhythmic repetition.** The middle paragraph is one long sentence: "you did JVC, I did the bare minimum to get through my Master's, you kicked ass in Med School, we raised a dog out of wedlock, you tried to kill me with the amount of stress caused by the Match..." Each clause carries a punchline. The form is "you/I/we did X, you/I/we did X."
- **Trust the audience with in-jokes.** "The very feasible plan that just left off Dante" — Dante is never identified (the dog?). Doesn't pause to explain. Readers/listeners either get it or they don't, and that's fine.
- **Alternating registers in lists.** The four-vow list goes: heartfelt → ironic → ironic → heartfelt. Doesn't lock into one tone; rotates intentionally.
- **Self-aware contradictions, named explicitly.** "At the risk of a slight contradiction, I also promise to always ask if you want my feedback or a solution before offering it." Acknowledges the tension rather than smoothing it over. Usable in articles when a framework has an internal trade-off — name it, don't hide it.

## The single line that captures the voice best

> "I promise to try really, really, really hard to be excited about the prospect of spending time and money with you on very reasonable activities like vacation, dinners out, and even Target."

Everything's in here: the conversational rhythm, the "very reasonable activities" (mock-formal qualifier doing comic work), the structural surprise of "and even Target" landing at the end of a list that started with vacation. This is the move to study.

# Original text (verbatim, as provided)

> There was a point in time where you used to look at multi-year engagements and go "yuck. I never want to do that." So I'm very sorry that it's taken us nearly 3 years to get to this point, and I am proud of you for overcoming that feeling and sticking it out with me.
>
> Two years ago when we canceled our original wedding without any sense of a future plan, I was incredibly sad. At that time I wanted nothing more than to show the world how much you meant to me, but the world clearly had different plans. We spent a lot of time thinking about eloping and we created many contingencies that could have worked, including one very feasible plan that just left off Dante, but all of these options felt wrong. We've always known that while today is very much about us, it would not be right without our families who molded us and our friends who have supported us.
>
> I have now known you for over a quarter of my life and when we met, 21 year-old Gio had a lot of room for growth. I was only just learning that it's not fashionable to wear running shoes if you do not have any intention of running. I had also just learned that if you put a shot of donut vodka into Pepsi, it turns into vanilla Pepsi. I had no idea who I wanted to be, nor what I wanted to do, but I had this unshaking feeling that I wanted to experience it all with you.
>
> Since that time, we've gone through a year of long distance while you did JVC, I did the bare minimum to get through my Master's, you kicked ass in Med School, we raised a dog out of wedlock, you tried to kill me with the amount of stress caused by the Match, you watched me fully embrace my truest Silicon Valley nerd and all of the weird calls, social gatherings that are functionally just team meetings, and late night grinds that come with it, and now you literally save lives and work grueling hours all in the service of others.
>
> Through all of this, our priorities and focus have never really changed. We've always made time for each other and the people we care about. You've created a home that is never off limits to people who need a quick place to rest, or a late night McDonald's run. Unless I'm actively writing code, our place has become a haven for people to congregate.
>
> I have loved all of these experiences with you. You are my partner in crime, my person, someone who shares my beliefs and priorities, and the person who taught me to believe in myself. I didn't know who I wanted to be, but now I know I just want to be someone who is worthy of you. Luckily for me, you continue to remind me quite frequently and I am incredibly grateful for your presence in my life.
>
> In order to continue this successful partnership:
> - I promise to love and support you, and to remind you of how wonderful, kind, and caring you are, even when you don't want to see it in yourself
> - At the risk of a slight contradiction, I also promise to always ask if you want my feedback or a solution before offering it
> - I promise to try really, really, really hard to be excited about the prospect of spending time and money with you on very reasonable activities like vacation, dinners out, and even Target
> - I promise to be yours and to treat you and the people we care about with the same compassion and empathy that you effortlessly maintain
>
> I love you. I look forward to no longer being a liar when I tell people you are my wife, and I am excited to dance and laugh with you tonight and during many more occasions to come.
