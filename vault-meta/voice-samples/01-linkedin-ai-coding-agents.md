**Audience naming:** explicitly addresses "people who know the barrier is gone but don't know where to start." Doesn't gesture vaguely at "many people" or "the industry."

**Personality through specifics:** "retro arcade vibes with neon colors," "space invaders-style particle effects," "RPG-themed skill trees." Personality leaks in through *what the author actually asked for*, not adjectives.

**Tactical lists:** four numbered beginner recommendations, each with a single-line elaboration. Tight, screenshot-able.

**Closing contrast pair:** "AI coding agents handle the syntax. You handle the outcomes." Parallel structure, designed to be quoted.

**Opening move:** contrarian thesis as the lead — "Software has always just been if-this-then-that thinking wrapped in special syntax." Doesn't ramp up; states the punch upfront.
