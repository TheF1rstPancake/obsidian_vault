# Guide: The solution design template

This guide has two parts. The paid page is a complete worked example for a fictitious support-triage problem. The blank template sits alongside it as a separate page you can copy into your own doc.

For SEs, SCs, and anyone who needs to turn discovery into a clear recommendation. The posture behind the structure — help the customer before you try to be right — is in [Are You Trying to Be Right, or Help the Customer?](/are-you-trying-to-be-right-or-help-the-customer/).

## What's inside

- **Worked example:** customer problem first, then solution-agnostic requirements, then the recommendation
- **Blank template:** a copyable version of the same structure
- **Practical follow-through:** responsibilities, timing, and open questions

The document's closing lifetime is short. Its post-sale lifetime is not. Implementation handoffs, scope disputes, expansion conversations, and renewal QBRs are where a good solution-agnostic requirements section earns its keep — both parties can still point at the shared agreement months later.
