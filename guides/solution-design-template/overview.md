# Guide: The solution design template

This fill-in-the-blank guide helps you understand a customer's problem before introducing your product. It starts with four boxes about the customer's world, turns them into a fifth box of solution-agnostic requirements, and only then bridges to the solution you recommend.

For SEs, SCs, and anyone who needs to turn discovery into a clear recommendation.

## What's inside

- **Four customer boxes:** current state, problems, goals and objectives, and ideal solution
- **A fifth bridge box:** a table of in-scope and out-of-scope requirements with no vendor or product language
- **A recommended solution:** a direct mapping from each in-scope requirement to your approach, including any gaps
- **Responsibilities, timing, and open questions:** the practical details needed to act on the recommendation
