# Guide: The solution design template

A fill-in-the-blank solution design document built to outlive the sale. The same artifact that closes the deal becomes the post-sale team's defense of scope at implementation, expansion, and renewal — so that a year later, after the AE has rotated and the implementation team has handed off, someone can still answer "what did we actually promise this customer?" It's for SEs, CSMs, and anyone who inherits an account and has to reconstruct its scope from Slack threads.

## What's inside

- **Header** — customer, owners, and a revision history that doubles as the spine of the document
- **Section 1 — Business context** — the problem, what success looks like, and who owns the outcome
- **Section 2 — Technical environment** — the current state implementation will check against reality
- **Section 3 — Solution scope** — in scope, explicitly out of scope, and deferred
- **Section 4 — Implementation commitments** — who does what, by when, and the dependencies between sides
- **Section 5 — Acceptance criteria** — how you know it worked, agreed before build
- **Section 6 — Renewal protection** — scheduled reviews and a scope drift log that turn a closing artifact into an operating one
- **Section 7 — Open items / decisions needed** — the honest list of what isn't resolved yet
