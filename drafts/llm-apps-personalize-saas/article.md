# Headless apps aren't new — LLMs are just the personalization layer over APIs

> Stub. Seed thoughts captured 2026-05-08. Awaiting voice recordings to flesh out.

## The thesis

"Why use Salesforce at all? I can just let Claude rebuild it." This take is technically correct on a certain level — and deeply incomplete. What knowledge workers have always wanted is the same thing engineers have always wanted from SaaS: software that (a) solves a core need and (b) bends to your nuances. LLMs make (b) feel attainable for the first time at the knowledge-worker layer. But they only feel that way **because someone else already built the core (a)**. Headless tooling and APIs aren't new. The novel part is that LLMs lower the activation energy for non-engineers to compose them.

## The contrarian line

You can only DIY *because* someone else built the primitives. The LLM is a personalization layer, not a foundation.
