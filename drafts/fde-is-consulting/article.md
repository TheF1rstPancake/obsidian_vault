# Forward Deployed Engineers are consultants with a better title

> Stub. Seed thoughts captured from a brain-dump conversation on 2026-05-07. Awaiting voice recordings to flesh out.

## The thesis

The "Forward Deployed Engineer" title is having a moment. Strip the branding and the role looks a lot like consulting: client-embedded, scoped engagements, hours pressure, your output measured by the customer's outcome rather than internal product wins. That's not a knock — consulting is a real craft — but anyone chasing the title because it sounds more engineer-y than consultant-y is in for a surprise.
