# Breadth of features used to be a moat. Now it's a liability.

> Stub. Seed thoughts captured 2026-05-08 (split out from the headless-apps thread). Awaiting voice recordings to flesh out.

## The thesis

"Look — no matter what you need, we can handle it" used to be a winning sales line. Buyers liked optionality; nobody got fired for picking the platform with the most features. That logic is inverting. Buyers are now opinionated — they know what they need and they want a tool that does *that* part well, with the flexibility to bend to their nuance. Tools that are wide and shallow lose to tools that are narrow and deep — *especially* when the narrow tool exposes clean APIs that LLMs can compose into the rest of the workflow.
