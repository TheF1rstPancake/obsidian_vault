# I built a voice-memo-to-Substack pipeline so I'd actually publish

> Stub. Seed thoughts captured 2026-05-07. Awaiting voice recordings to flesh out.

## The thesis

I had article ideas for years and never published. The bottleneck wasn't writing — it was *capture*. By the time I sat down at a keyboard, the spark was gone. So I built a pipeline: voice memo on a walk → Whisper transcribes → Claude shapes a draft → Obsidian for refinement → Substack. The act of building it taught me more about my own bottleneck than years of "I should write more" did.

## What to flesh out in future recordings

- The actual stack: Syncthing, Whisper, Claude, Obsidian, cron — and why each piece earned its spot
- The "many recordings, one article" insight (notes.md as lossless source, article.md as polished output)
- The motivation collapse the first time around — built it, didn't use it for two months — and what changed
- The meta-lesson: when capture is the bottleneck, no amount of writing advice helps; you have to lower the activation energy
