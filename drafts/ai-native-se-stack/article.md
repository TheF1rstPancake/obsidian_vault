# The AI-native SE stack: scaling sub-linearly with headcount

> Stub. Seed thoughts captured from a brain-dump conversation on 2026-05-07. Awaiting voice recordings to flesh out.

## The thesis

If I had a larger team to support today, I already know the stack I'd use to scale SE operations *without* growing headcount linearly with the rest of the company. The internal enablement, support, and operations workflows that were brutally hard to maintain pre-AI are now mostly a wiring problem — concepts in the right order.

## The frame to lead with

Don't lead with the tool list. Lead with **which workflows break first as the company grows, and in what order to AI-enable them.** The tools are the supporting cast.
