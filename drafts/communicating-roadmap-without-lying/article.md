## The thesis (rough)

In early-stage companies, the gap between *what we plan to build* and *what we will actually ship* is enormous. The roadmap is the most demanded artifact in customer conversations and the most dangerous one. Say too much and you've quietly signed a contract you can't deliver. Say too little and you lose the deal to a competitor who promised the moon.

There is a posture between those two — honest about direction, vague enough about timing to stay credible six months later. That posture is a learnable skill.
