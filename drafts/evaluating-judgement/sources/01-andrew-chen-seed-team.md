## Key claims

- **T-shaped people**: broad competence across domains plus deep expertise in one. Enables cross-functional conversations while providing specialized value.
- **Missionaries vs mercenaries**: assess whether candidates believe in the mission or are purely financially motivated. Mercenaries create "revenue-oriented culture that leads to weird company vision problems."
- **Execution focus over philosophy**: prioritize doers; warns against candidates "more interested in reading blogs and indulging themselves intellectually than really working hard."
- **Test real work outside the stated skillset**: engineering interviews should include product/design questions, not just coding trivia. The signal you're after is broader than role-defined competence.
- **Three-day working interviews** as a proxy for actual collaboration — extended evaluation beats compressed signal.
- **Soft factors weighted equally** with skillset: work style, decision-making process, pacing, hours, motivation source, stage preference.
